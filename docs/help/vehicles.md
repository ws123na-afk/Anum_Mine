# Vehicles

Vehicles keeps your cars and business vehicles: fuel, trips, service and fees, the odometer, reminders before expiry dates, and what each vehicle costs per month and per kilometre. Costs are your Finance payments linked to a vehicle, so a payment is never counted twice.

## Add a vehicle

1. Open **Vehicles** in the sidebar and click **Add vehicle**.
2. Choose **Personal** (only you see it) or **Business** (members of this workspace see it).
3. Enter the **Registration**, **Country**, **Fuel type** and, if you like, the make, model, VIN, tank size, the odometer now and the insurance, registration and inspection dates.
4. Click **Save**.

## Log fuel and link its payment

1. Open the vehicle and click **Add fuel**. Enter the time, litres, pump total and the odometer. Tick **Full tank** for a full fill.
2. Click **Payment** on the fill. Pick the payment from your Finance ledger (Apple Pay or the bank SMS already recorded it), or choose **Paid in cash** to record it once in Finance.
3. The vehicle now shows the cost. Linking adds no new expense.

## Trips, service and the odometer

1. On **Trips**, click **Add trip** with the start and end odometer, or let the iPhone Shortcut start and end trips when CarPlay connects and disconnects.
2. On **Service**, log oil, tyres, repairs, insurance, tolls, parking and fines, with the next due date or kilometres.
3. On **Odometer**, add a reading. The odometer never goes down: if a reading was wrong, tick **This is a correction** and write the reason.

The **Report** tab shows cost per km, consumption (only from one full fill to the next), fuel prices and anything unusual. **Details** has edit, archive, delete and **Export CSV**.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members: add vehicles and log records. A business vehicle is changed by the person who added it or a workspace owner; a personal vehicle is only yours.
- Linking a payment needs Finance access, and you only ever pick from your own payments.
- Viewers: cannot use Vehicles. Ask an owner to change your role.
