# AI Chat

AI Chat is a conversation with the Assistant or one of your agents. Answers stream in as they are written, can use your files, mail and memories, and follow-ups remember the conversation.

## Ask a question

1. Open **AI Chat** in the sidebar.
2. To talk to an agent instead of the Assistant, choose it under **Talk to**.
3. Press **New chat**, type in the box (Ask the assistant...) and press **Send** or Enter. Shift+Enter adds a new line.
4. Press **Stop** to end an answer early. If an answer fails, press **Retry**.
5. Press **Show what this chat used** to see the sources, memories and tasks behind the chat.

## Work with answers and messages

1. Open the actions menu next to an answer to copy it, press **Regenerate answer** or delete it.
2. Open the menu next to your own message and press **Edit and resend** to change the question.
3. Rate an answer with **Helpful** or **Not helpful**, or press **Edit to teach** to show ANUM how you would have written it.

## Find and organize conversations

1. Type in **Search conversations** to find a chat by its title or any message.
2. Open **Conversation actions** for a chat and choose **Rename**, **Pin to top** or **Delete conversation**.
3. From the same menu, choose **Copy conversation as Markdown** or **Download as Markdown (.md)**.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members can ask, start and delete conversations.
- Viewers cannot use the assistant; the page says that a workspace owner must give them the member role.
- Each person sees only their own conversations. When the monthly model budget is used up, new questions are refused until it resets or an owner raises it.
