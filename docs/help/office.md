# ANUM MINE headquarters

ANUM MINE draws every workspace you can open as a building on a campus. Inside a building you see its floors, rooms and desks, and the agents seated at them. Counts and statuses come from real records: agent tasks, approvals and live agent meetings. Where no record exists the page says "Presence unavailable" or "Not connected" and never guesses.

## Look around the campus

1. Open **ANUM MINE** in the sidebar.
2. Drag to turn the view, scroll to zoom, hold **Shift** while dragging to move. The compass and the zoom buttons on the right do the same.
3. Choose **3D**, **Top** or **List** above the scene. **List** is a plain list of every building and room, and works with a keyboard and a screen reader.
4. Select a building label to read its summary, then press it again, or click **Open** in the summary, to go inside.

## Work inside a building

1. Pick a floor with the floor arrows or the floor menu. **All floors** shows the whole stack.
2. Select a room to open its panel with the desks, who sits there and what they are doing.
3. Select an agent card to see their open tasks and to open their profile.
4. **Office tools** opens Manage spaces, Live workstations, Agent meeting sessions, Reception and the Guided visit.

## Who can do this

- Everyone with access to a workspace can see its building, rooms and agent status.
- Owners and workspace managers can change rooms and desks in **Manage spaces**.
- Commerce and tyre panels at the bottom show numbers only when that module is connected and you may read it. Otherwise they say who can set it up.

## Learn more

Open **Office tools**, then **Manage spaces**, to create the rooms and desks a building shows.
