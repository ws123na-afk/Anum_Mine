# ANUM MINE headquarters

ANUM MINE draws every workspace you can open as a building on a campus. Inside a building you see its floors, rooms and desks, and the agents seated at them. Counts and statuses come from real records: agent tasks, approvals and live agent meetings. Where no record exists the page says "Presence unavailable" or "Not connected" and never guesses.

## Look around the campus

1. Open **ANUM MINE** in the sidebar.
2. Drag to turn the view, scroll to zoom, hold **Shift** while dragging to move. The compass and the zoom buttons on the right do the same.
3. Choose **3D**, **Top** or **List** above the scene. **List** is a plain list of every building and room, and works with a keyboard and a screen reader.
4. Select a building label to read its summary, then press it again, or click **Open** in the summary, to go inside.
5. Use the sun and moon buttons to switch between day and night. Zoom in on a building to read the sign over its entrance: ANUM MINE and the building's name. The type of building follows the icon the owner picked for it (a truck or factory is a warehouse, a chart or briefcase is an office tower). On a slow device the scene lowers its detail by itself.

## Move quickly between offices

- The **Quick views** strip under the scene jumps to the campus aerial, to any building you can open, and to the rooms of the open building. The camera flies there; with reduced motion it jumps. The current stop is outlined. **Collapse** hides the strip.
- Press the stop of the selected building again to go inside. A building you cannot open is shown locked.
- Thumbnails are snapshots of the scene taken the first time you look at a place in this browser tab. Until then a plain icon stands in.
- On the campus the cards show a building's type, floors and rooms, and its agent count. With many buildings the cards shrink: the selected, the open and the executive building keep a full card, the busiest fill six, and the rest show a name and a count, then only an icon (the name appears on hover and focus). No card disappears.
- If your workspace's office has several buildings (for example after **Set up my campus**), each one stands on the campus as its own building, labelled with the workspace name. Its card counts the agents assigned to its desks. Buildings of other workspaces you belong to stand beside them, one each.
- **Quick moves** (right side) start the office tour, open meetings or the calendar, and suggest "Take me to" any building or room that exists.
- **Hide side panels** (above the scene) folds the floor list and the right column away so the scene uses the whole width; **Show side panels** brings them back. The choice is remembered in this browser.
- Arrow keys move between quick views and between floors; Home and End jump to the first and last.

## Work inside a building

1. Pick a floor from the **Floors** list on the left (top floor first, with a plan thumbnail and its room types). Collapse it to a slim column if you want more room for the scene. On a narrow screen it opens as a drawer.
2. On the right, **Floor plan** shows the saved rooms of the floor. **2D** is the top-down view, **3D** the perspective view. Select a room on the plan to open it.
3. **People at their posts** lists the agents assigned to desks on the floor with their real status. A desk without an assignment is not listed. An agent with no presence record reads **Not connected** (or **Presence unavailable** when the server cannot say), and an agent that is private to someone else shows no name.
4. The old floor arrows and menu stay for small screens, and **All floors** shows the whole stack.

5. Select a room to open its panel with the desks, who sits there and what they are doing.
6. Select an agent card or a name in the list to see their open tasks and to open their profile.
7. **Office tools** opens Manage spaces, Live workstations, Agent meeting sessions, Reception and the Guided visit.

## Staff every empty desk

1. Open **Office tools**, then **Staff my campus** (also in **Organisation gaps**).
2. Read the plan. For every empty desk it names the agent that would be created, for example "Orders Desk, Orders 2". The totals show how many desks get an agent, how many stay empty and why, and the largest possible monthly cost if every new agent were turned on.
3. Untick buildings or rooms you do not want, set a limit for this run, or press **Keep empty** on a desk you want left empty.
4. Press **Staff desks**. Only a workspace owner can, and only with a click. Every agent is created paused, can read and draft but not send or pay, and has a monthly cost cap.
5. Open **Paused agents** to turn one agent on, or select several, check the cost cap total and turn them on.

A workspace can have 200 agents at most. Desks past that stay empty and the plan says how many.

## Who can do this

- Everyone with access to a workspace can see its building, rooms and agent status.
- Owners and workspace managers can change rooms and desks in **Manage spaces**.
- Workspace owners staff desks and turn the new agents on. Everyone else can read the plan.
- Commerce and tyre panels at the bottom show numbers only when that module is connected and you may read it. Otherwise they say who can set it up.
- A room opens the module of its department, which an owner sets in the builder: Finance opens Finance, Sales opens Sales and CRM, Support opens the inbox, Operations opens the live flow, Tyre engineering opens Tyre Planner, Marketing opens Marketing, Technology opens Developer mode, Research opens the Knowledge Base, Legal opens Documents, Compliance opens Governance, Security opens the Audit log and HR opens Members and roles. Procurement and Custom have no module: the room says "No module connected for this department" and offers **Organisation gaps**. The same link is in the room list.
- A meeting can be held in a meeting room. Choose the room when you create the meeting under **Office tools**, **Agent meeting sessions**, or select a meeting room and press **Start a meeting here** (owners). While the meeting is active the room shows **In a meeting** on its label, in the list and in its panel, with the title and the agents that joined, and its link opens that live meeting. When the meeting ends, the mark goes away.
- **Set up my campus** can be run again: a campus room that has no department, or the custom one, gets the department of the standard list so it can open its module. A department you chose yourself is never changed.
- A reception room shows its desks and assigned agents only. There is no visitor register yet, and the office shows no visitor names.
