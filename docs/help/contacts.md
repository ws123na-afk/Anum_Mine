# Contacts

Contacts shows every person once, however many lists they are on: the Phone calls address book, Business contacts and WhatsApp customers. Each person carries their numbers and addresses, where the records came from, what each module says about contacting them, and one do-not-contact mark per channel that calls, e-mail, WhatsApp and Business outreach all obey.

## Find a person and see who may contact them

1. Open **Contacts** in the sidebar. Type a name, organisation, number or address under **Search** and press **Find**; filter by **Came from** or **Do not contact**, and sort by name or last change.
2. Click a name. The person page lists the **Linked records** with a link into Phone calls, Business or WhatsApp, and your own mailbox contact if you have one.
3. Under **Who may contact them, per channel**, each card says whether the channel is open and why: consent to AI calls, the Business consent basis, whether the customer wrote first on WhatsApp, the opt-out list and the consent records.
4. Press **Rebuild now** to bring in contacts added since the last nightly build.

## Mark a person do not contact

1. On the person page, press **Mark do not contact** on a channel (or pick **All channels**) and write the reason.
2. From then on a browser or AI call, an Email Center send, a WhatsApp reply or a Business outreach message to that channel is refused with the reason, until a workspace owner clears the mark with a reason.
3. The modules' own flags and the Contact opt-outs list are not changed; they still apply as before.

## Decide a possible duplicate or merge two people

1. Open the **Possible duplicates** tab. Each pair shares a number or an address under different names; nothing merges on its own.
2. Press **Keep** on the record to keep: the other person's numbers, addresses, linked records and marks move to it and the other record is deleted. Press **Different people** to keep both.
3. On a person page, **Merge with...** finds another person by name or number and merges them in the same way.

## Who can do what

- Workspace members and owners read Contacts and change people, mark do not contact, decide duplicates and rebuild.
- Workspace owners also clear a do-not-contact mark, delete a person and export the CSV.
- Viewers cannot open Contacts: it holds personal data from the four lists. Ask a workspace owner to make you a member.
