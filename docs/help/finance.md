# Finance

Finance is your own private ledger: accounts, transactions, bills, debts, goals and holdings, built from the statements you import. Nothing here moves money: there is no pay, transfer or trade button.

## Add an account

1. Open **Finance** in the sidebar and click the **Accounts** tab.
2. Click **Add account**.
3. Enter the **Name**, pick the **Type** and **Currency**, and optionally the **Bank or provider** and opening balance.
4. Click **Save**.

## Import a bank statement

1. Click the **Import** tab.
2. Choose the **Account** and the **CSV file**. Leave **Layout** on **Detect automatically** unless your bank is listed.
3. Click **Preview** and check the column mapping and sample rows. Change a column and click **Preview again** if needed.
4. Click the import button, for example **Import 42 rows**. Rows already imported are skipped.
5. Made a mistake? Click **Undo import** next to the file under **Earlier imports**.

## Find and fix transactions

1. Click the **Transactions** tab.
2. Type in **Search descriptions and notes**, or open **Filters** to narrow by account, category, **Money**, **From** and **To**, then click **Search**.
3. Click the pencil button on a row to edit it, for example to change its category, or click **Add transaction** for a cash entry.
4. Click **Export CSV** to download what you see.

Use **Bills**, **Debts** and **Goals** the same way: **Add bill**, **Add debt** or **Add goal**, fill in the form and click **Save**.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members: use Finance for their own money. Every record is private to the person who made it: a workspace owner cannot read a member's ledger.
- Viewers: cannot use Finance. Ask an owner to change your role.
