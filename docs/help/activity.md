# Activity

Activity lists every action ANUM took without asking, newest first. From here you can undo what can be reversed, make a kind of action ask again, and open **Autonomy** to choose how much ANUM does on its own.

## Review and undo actions

1. Open **Activity** in the sidebar, under **More**.
2. Filter by **Category**, **How it acted** or **Status**, or search summaries, tools and templates. Click **Clear filters** to see everything again.
3. Click **Undo** on an action while it can still be reversed.
4. Click **Always ask for this** to make that kind of action wait for approval from now on. It then shows **Asks from now on**.
5. Click **Load more** for older actions. When ANUM acts, a notice also offers **Undo** and **View activity**.

## Decide on suggestions to ask less

1. At the top of Activity, **Suggestions to ask less** lists actions you approved several times.
2. Click **Accept** to let ANUM act and notify you next time, or **Keep asking**. ANUM never raises its own autonomy.

## Set autonomy levels

1. Click **Autonomy settings** on Activity, or open **Autonomy** in the sidebar under **More**.
2. Under **Level by area**, pick **Ask always**, **Act and notify** or **Act silently** for each area. Money, prices, stock, deleting, merging code, new recipients and bulk sends always ask.
3. Under **Thresholds and limits**, set amounts, **Approved templates**, **Daily caps**, **Quiet hours** and blocked recipients or domains, then click **Save autonomy**.
4. Under **Rules for exact actions**, fill **Tool**, **Template (optional)** and **Level**, then click **Add rule**.
5. Organization administrators set **Organization guardrails** and click **Save guardrails**.

## Who can do this

- Every active member, viewers included: read Activity and the autonomy settings.
- Members and owners: **Always ask for this** and "Ask always" rules.
- An owner, or the person whose task did it: **Undo**.
- Owners only: accept suggestions, change levels, thresholds and other rules, and remove rules.
- Organization administrators only: change the organization guardrails.
