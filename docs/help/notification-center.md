# Notifications

The Notifications page lists everything that reached you in the app: work assigned to you, comments, overdue reminders, calendar reminders, approval reminders and notices from modules such as Finance, Vehicles and the Document vault. Your notifications are private to you.

## Open the page

1. Click the bell in the top bar, then **See all notifications**.
2. Or open **Notifications** in the sidebar, under **More**.
3. The list shows 20 at a time, newest first. Use **Next** and **Previous** at the bottom to page.

## Filter and open

1. Click **All**, **Unread** or **Read** to choose which ones you see.
2. Click a kind, such as **Assigned to you** or **Calendar reminders**, to see only that kind. The number after it is how many you have of that kind.
3. Click a notification's title to open it. A work item opens in Team; a reminder or notice opens the page it is about. Opening it marks it read.

## Read, unread and delete

1. Click the open envelope button to mark one read, or the closed envelope to mark it unread again so it counts on the bell.
2. Click **Mark all read** at the top to clear every unread one at once.
3. Click the bin button to delete one, then **Delete** to confirm. It is gone for good; the work item or record it points to is not changed.

## Who can do this

- Every role can see, mark and delete their own notifications. Nobody else can, owners included.
- How notifications reach you (in the app, by email, on your phone) is set in **Settings**, **Notifications**.
