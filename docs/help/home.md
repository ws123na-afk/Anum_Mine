# Home

Home is your overview of the day: approvals waiting, tasks, unread mail and WhatsApp, today's meetings, automation alerts and AI budget use. To give ANUM an order, use ANUM Command instead.

## Check what needs you today

1. Open **Home** in the sidebar.
2. Read **Today at a glance**. Each card opens its own area, for example Tasks or Approvals.
3. Read **Today's focus** and **Recent activity** for urgent items and the latest changes.
4. Press **Refresh** to load the overview again.
5. Press **Give ANUM an order** to open ANUM Command.

## Decide on approvals and insights

1. Under **Waiting for your decision**, read the tool, the risk and the payload hash on each card.
2. Press **Approve** or **Deny**. If the decision is refused, the reason shows on the card.
3. Under **ANUM noticed**, press the insight's action button, **Later** to hide it for 24 hours, or **Dismiss**.
4. A dismissed or snoozed insight comes back on its own while it is still true.

## Pin what you open every day

1. Under **Pinned**, press **Edit pins**.
2. Under **What to pin**, choose **A part of ANUM** or **A web app (https link)**.
3. Pick the part, or type the link and a name, then press **Add a pin**.
4. Use **Move up**, **Move down** or **Remove** to arrange your pins, then press **Done**.
5. You can keep up to 24 pins.

## Who can do this

- Every active member of the workspace, viewers included, can open Home, dismiss or snooze insights and edit their own pins.
- Approving or denying needs a workspace owner, or a person an approval route names.
- Only workspace owners see AI budget use. A part your role cannot read says so, and the rest of Home still loads.
