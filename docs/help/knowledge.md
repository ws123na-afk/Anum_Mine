# Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base has two tabs: **Memories**, the notes and facts ANUM remembers from tasks and your chats, and **Collections**, groups of documents and notes you can search and ask questions about.

## Create a collection and add to it

1. Open **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar and click the **Collections** tab.
2. Click **New collection**, enter a **Name**, choose **Who can see it** and click **Create collection**.
3. Open the collection and click **Add**.
4. Pick **Upload**, **Write a note** or **From Files**, fill in the form, add **Tags** if you like, and click **Upload document**, **Save note** or **Add file**.

## Ask the knowledge base

1. On the **Collections** tab, type in **Question or search words**.
2. Under **Look in**, choose **All collections I can see** or only the open collection.
3. Click **Ask** for an answer with numbered **Sources**, or **Search** for matching passages.
4. If an item shows that indexing failed, click **Re-index** on its collection.

## Manage memories

1. Click the **Memories** tab.
2. Type in **Find notes by text** and click **Search**. Pick **All memories** or **From my chats**.
3. Click **Add memory**, choose the **Task**, type the **Memory** and click **Add memory**.
4. Click the bin button (**Delete memory**) on a row, then **Delete**. **Delete all from my chats** removes every memory from your chats.
5. Click the **Chat memory** chip to turn **Remember things from my chats** on or off.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members: create collections, add documents and notes, re-index, ask, and add or delete memories.
- Viewers: read collections and memories and search them.
- Renaming or deleting a collection is for its creator, or an owner for a shared one. A private collection and memories from your chats are seen only by you.
