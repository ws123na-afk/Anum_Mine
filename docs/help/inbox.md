# Inbox

The Inbox shows the Gmail, Outlook and Zoho Mail mailboxes connected to this workspace as one list, sorted by category and priority. ANUM drafts replies, and nothing is sent until an owner approves the exact message.

## Connect a mailbox

1. Open **Inbox** in the sidebar under **More**.
2. With no mailbox yet, the page shows **Connect a mailbox**. Otherwise click the mailbox status chip in the header to open **Mailboxes**.
3. Tick **Only visible to me (a private mailbox)** if others should not see it.
4. Click **Connect Gmail / Google Workspace**, **Connect Outlook / Microsoft 365** or **Connect Zoho Mail** and sign in at the provider.
5. Use **Sync** on a mailbox, or **Sync all** in the header, to fetch new mail.

## Find and read a message

1. Type in **Search sender, subject, text** and press **Search**.
2. Narrow the list with **Mailbox**, **Category** and **Priority**, or tick **Unread** or **Archived**.
3. Click a message to read its text, attachments and AI summary.
4. Use **Mark read**, **Mark unread** or **Archive** on the open message. **Clear filters** shows everything again.

## Reply with an approved draft

1. Open the message and click **Summarise and suggest a reply**, then **Use as draft**. Or type optional instructions and click **Draft with AI**, or click **Write my own reply**.
2. Check **To**, **Subject** and **Message**, then click **Save draft**.
3. Click **Ask approval to send**.
4. An owner checks the payload hash and clicks **Approve and send**, here or in Approvals. If you change the draft, save it and ask again.

## Who can do this

- Owners: everything, including connecting and disconnecting mailboxes and approving sending.
- Members: read, search, mark read, archive, draft and ask to send. They cannot connect mailboxes or approve sending.
- Viewers: cannot open the workspace mailboxes.
- A private mailbox is seen only by the person who connected it.
