# Calls & Meetings

Calls & Meetings turns the calls you import into a summary, decisions, action items and a follow-up email. No bot joins your meetings: you upload a recording or transcript, or import from Zoom or Teams.

## Import a meeting

1. Open **Calls & Meetings** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Import a meeting**.
3. Choose **Transcript file**, **Recording**, **Zoom** or **Teams**.
4. For a file, set the **Title** and **Language**, then **Choose a transcript file** or **Choose a recording**. For Zoom or Teams, click **Connect Zoom** or **Connect Microsoft Teams** once, then **Import** next to a meeting.
5. The summary appears when it is ready. Find meetings later with **Search titles, summaries and transcripts** or the **Status** filter.

## Turn action items into tasks

1. Open a meeting from the list.
2. Read the **Summary**, **Decisions** and **Action items**. Click **Summarize again** if the transcript changed.
3. Edit an item to set its **Owner** and **Due date**.
4. Click **Create task** on an item. Running the task follows the workspace approval policy.

## Send the follow-up email

1. In the open meeting, go to **Follow-up email**.
2. Check **To**, **Subject** and **Message**, then click **Save draft**.
3. Click **Send with approval** to send from a connected Inbox mailbox once an owner approves it, or use **Copy** or **Open in mail app**.

## Who can do this

- Viewers: read meetings, notes and transcripts.
- Members: also import meetings, connect Zoom or Teams, and edit, summarize again or delete the meetings they imported.
- Owners: also edit and delete anyone's meetings, and approve the follow-up email.

## Who can read a live agent meeting

Only workspace owners read the transcript, decisions and downloads of a live agent meeting. Members and viewers still see that it happened, its title, status and who is in it. A monthly budget shared by all live agent meetings pauses them when it is used up (see Budgets).
