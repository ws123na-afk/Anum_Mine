# Commerce

Commerce brings your sales channels (Amazon, noon and your own store) into one place: sales by channel, orders, the catalogue with tyre details, returns and alerts. Price and stock changes wait for an approval before anything changes on a channel.

## Find an order

1. Open **Commerce** in the sidebar and click the **Orders** tab.
2. Pick a **Status** and a **Channel**, or type an **Order number or SKU** and click **Search**.
3. Click an order to see its lines and status. Only owners see the buyer's name and address.

## Change a price or stock level

1. Click the **Catalogue** tab and search by SKU, title, brand or size.
2. On a product's listing, click **Change price** or **Change stock**.
3. Enter the new value and click **Ask for approval**. Note the payload hash shown.
4. Click **Open Approvals**. An owner approves the change there, and only then does the channel update.

## Connect a sales channel

1. Click the **Connections** tab and click **Add connection**.
2. Choose the channel, enter its credentials and click **Connect**. Credentials are never shown again.
3. Click **Test connection** to check it, and **Sync now** to fetch orders and listings straight away.
4. Watch the **Alerts** tab for low stock and price changes, and click **Acknowledge** once handled.

## Who can do this

- Owners: everything, including connecting, changing and removing channels, alert rules and seeing buyer details.
- Members: edit the catalogue, sync now, acknowledge alerts and propose price and stock changes.
- Viewers: read the overview, orders (without buyer details), catalogue, returns, alerts and connection health.
- Price and stock changes always need an owner's approval in Approvals.
