# Assistants

Department assistants are agent profiles for Sales, Support, Operations, Finance and Tyre engineering. Each one uses only its allowed tools at its autonomy level, and every action still goes through tool policy and approvals.

## Set up an assistant

1. Open **Assistants** in the sidebar and click **New assistant**.
2. Click **Use template** next to a department, or **Start blank**. A department that already has an assistant shows **Already set up**.
3. Fill **Name**, **Department**, **Autonomy** (**Observe only**, **Suggest, a person approves** or **Act on low-risk steps**), **Allowed tools** and **Instructions**.
4. Add **Routing keywords**, separated by commas. Under **Take new messages on arrival**, tick **Email** or **WhatsApp** to turn matching messages from a shared mailbox or the workspace WhatsApp number into its work automatically.
5. Leave **Turned on** ticked and click **Save**.

## Change, turn off or delete an assistant

1. Click an assistant's name, or its pencil button, to edit it, then click **Save**.
2. Click the power button: **Turn off** for an assistant that is on, **Turn on** for one that is off.
3. Click the bin button and confirm **Delete**. An assistant with open work cannot be deleted: reassign the work or turn it off instead.
4. The table shows each assistant's open work, overdue items and finished work. With more than four assistants, use **Search assistants**.

## Route a customer message

1. Open **Route an inbound message** at the bottom of the page.
2. Pick the **Channel**, fill **From**, **Subject** and **Message**.
3. Leave **Route to** on **Best match by keywords**, or pick an assistant.
4. Click **Route message**. The message becomes a work item for the matching assistant, or waits unassigned for a person. Click **Open** to see it in Team.

## Who can do this

- Every role, viewers included: see the assistants and their workload.
- Members and owners: route an inbound message.
- Owners only: create, change, turn off and delete assistants.
