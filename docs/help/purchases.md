# Purchases

Purchases keeps what you ordered online or in a shop: where it is, what you are sending back, the refunds you are owed and the warranty. The money is your Finance transactions linked to a purchase, so a payment is never counted twice and a refund is never counted as income.

## Add a purchase

1. Open **Purchases and Returns** in the sidebar and click **Track purchase** (or **Import order** to paste an order confirmation).
2. Choose **Personal** (only you see it) or **Business** (members of this workspace see it).
3. Enter the **Shop or seller**, the **Order number**, the order date and, if you like, the items, the shop total, the expected delivery, a label such as **Home** for where it goes, the return window and the warranty.
4. Click **Save**. From your iPhone you can instead share the order e-mail to the ANUM Shortcut.

## Follow the delivery

1. Open the purchase. On **Delivery**, pick the next status (confirmed, shipped, out for delivery, delivered) and click **Change status**. Every change is kept under **History**.
2. Click **Add shipment** with the carrier and tracking number. **Mark delivered** on a shipment updates the purchase.
3. Under **Details**, click **Payment** and pick the payment from your Finance ledger (Apple Pay or the bank SMS already recorded it), or record a cash payment once.

## Return an item and link the refund

1. On **Returns and refunds**, click **Ask for a return**, choose the items and quantities (or none for the whole order), the reason, the return date and the pickup.
2. Move the return on as the shop answers: approved, pickup scheduled, sent back, refund pending, or disputed.
3. When the money comes back, click **Link refund** and pick the refund from Finance (or record a cash refund). A partial refund marks the purchase partly refunded. The refund lowers what the purchase cost; it is never income.

ANUM reminds you, in the app only, about late deliveries, closing return windows, pickups, overdue refunds, ending warranties and missing confirmations. The reminders stop once a purchase is refunded, cancelled or archived.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members: add purchases, follow deliveries, add shipments and ask for returns. A business purchase is changed by the person who added it or a workspace owner; a personal purchase is only yours.
- Only the person who added a purchase links its payment and refunds, and only from their own Finance ledger. Others see the amounts only.
- Viewers: cannot use Purchases. Ask an owner to change your role.
