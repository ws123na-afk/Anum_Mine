# Settings

Settings has five tabs: **Workspace**, **Notifications**, **Language**, **Appearance** and **Privacy**.

## Manage this workspace

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar, under **More**. The **Workspace** tab opens first.
2. **This workspace** shows the name, Workspace ID, organization, creation date and your role.
3. To rename it, type the **Workspace name** and click **Save name**.
4. To make it read-only, click **Archive workspace**, type the workspace name to confirm, and click **Archive workspace** again. Click **Restore workspace** to undo it.
5. To add a workspace, fill **Name** under **Create another workspace** and click **Create workspace**, then **Switch to** it.
6. Lower on the tab: **Organisation name and logo**, the **Model gateway** (**Save model**, **Test connection**) and **Chat memory** (**Remember things from my chats**).

## Choose your notifications

1. Open the **Notifications** tab.
2. Tick what you want to hear about, such as **Approvals waiting for me**, **Tasks finished** or **Tasks failed**, and how: **Phone push** or **Desktop notifications**.
3. Click **Save notifications**. Under **Phone push**, click **Remove** next to a phone you no longer use.

## Set language and theme

1. On the **Language** tab, pick **Match my browser**, English or العربية. Arabic lays the interface out right to left.
2. On the **Appearance** tab, pick **Match my device**, **Light** or **Dark**.
3. Both choices are kept in this browser only.

## Download or delete your data

1. Open the **Privacy** tab to see what ANUM stores about you, what is **Private to you** and what is **Shared with the workspace**.
2. Click **Download my data** for a JSON file of your data.
3. To erase your private data, type the confirmation phrase shown and click **Delete my data**. This cannot be undone.

## Who can do this

- Everyone, viewers included: notifications, language, theme, chat memory and their own privacy tools.
- Workspace owners and organization administrators: rename, archive, restore and create workspaces.
- Workspace owners only: the model gateway. Organization administrators only: the organisation name and logo.
