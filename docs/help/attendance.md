# Attendance

Attendance is your own work log: when you arrived and left, at which site, your breaks and remote work, your hours by day, week and month, and a daily report of what you did. It is a personal log, not an employer-certified timesheet.

## Check in and out

1. Open **Attendance** in the sidebar. The **Today** tab shows whether you are checked in.
2. Choose the **Site** and click **Check in**. Click **Start break**, **End break** or **Check out** as your day goes.
3. Working from home? Click **Start remote work** and later **End remote work**.
4. Pressing a button twice never counts twice.

## Fix a missing or wrong record

1. Click the **Corrections** tab.
2. Choose **Add**, **Change** or **Remove**. For a change or removal, pick the record.
3. Enter what happened, the day, the time and the site, and write the **Reason**. A reason is always required.
4. Click **Save correction**. The old record is kept in the **Correction history**.

## Write a daily report and get your hours

1. Click the **Daily report** tab, pick the **Day** and fill in the tasks done, still to do, site visits, issues and notes. Attach photos with **Attach photos or files**.
2. Click **Save report**. Earlier reports are listed below with a search.
3. Click the **Reports** tab, choose **Week** or **Month**, and click **CSV** or **PDF** to download your hours.

Set up your profile, schedule, sites, location check in, sharing and iPhone Shortcuts on the **Settings** tab.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members: keep their own attendance. Nobody else sees it, a workspace owner included, unless you share it on the **Settings** tab. A person you share with can read it but never change it.
- Viewers: cannot use Attendance. Ask an owner to change your role.
