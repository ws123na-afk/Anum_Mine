# Call

Call is a voice call with the assistant: you talk, pause, and it answers aloud. You can turn on the camera to show it what you see. Nothing is approved, deleted or paid by voice.

## Start a call

1. Open **Call** in the sidebar.
2. Choose the **Call language** and how long to keep the **Transcript**: **Delete when the call ends**, **Keep for 30 days** or **Keep until deleted**.
3. Pick **Speech recognition** and **Reply voice** if the workspace offers more than the free device engines.
4. Click **Start call** and allow the microphone. Just talk, and pause when you are done.

## Use the controls during a call

1. **Mute** turns the microphone off. Tap the orb to stop a reply.
2. **Video** turns the camera on. Click **Send frame** to show one picture, or tick **Share a frame with each question**.
3. **Speaker** stops replies being spoken; they stay in **Chat**.
4. **Chat** shows the transcript, lets you type, and offers **Create task** when the assistant proposes one. **More** shows reply speed.

## End and save a call

1. Click **End call**.
2. In **Save this call?**, click **Save summary** to keep a summary and transcript as a conversation, or **End without saving**.
3. Click **Open conversation** to read it in AI Chat, or **New call** to start again.
4. Your earlier calls are listed under **Recent calls**; click one to read its transcript.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members: start calls, use the camera, create tasks and save summaries.
- Viewers: can read their own recent calls but cannot start or save a call.
- Your calls are private: only you see them.
