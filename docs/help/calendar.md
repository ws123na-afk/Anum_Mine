# Calendar

The Calendar is the workspace calendar: meetings, calls, reviews, reminders and focus time, with the next steps ANUM suggests from your calendar, email and approvals. Events live in ANUM; Google and Microsoft calendars are not synced yet.

## Add an event

1. Open **Calendar** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New event**.
3. Fill **Title**, **Type**, **Date**, **Starts** and **Ends**, and optionally **Place**, **Meeting link**, **Phone number**, **People** (one per line) and **Notes**.
4. Click **Add event**. ANUM does not send invitations.

## Find and manage events

1. On the **Agenda** tab, choose **Week** or **Day** and move with **Previous week**, **Today** and **Next week**.
2. Type in **Search title, place or people**, or tick **Show cancelled**.
3. Click an event to see its details, then **Join** a meeting link.
4. Use **Edit**, **Cancel event**, **Restore** or **Delete**. Set each person's reply under **People**.

## Act on next steps

1. Open the **Next steps** tab.
2. Read the suggestions: preparing for a meeting, following up on missing replies, or a summary after a meeting.
3. Click **Start task** to run a task that drafts only, or **Open** to see the related item. Anything sent waits for approval.

## Who can do this

- Every role, viewers included: see the calendar and the next steps.
- Members and owners: add events and start next-step tasks.
- The person who added an event, or a workspace owner: edit, cancel, delete it and record replies.
