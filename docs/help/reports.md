# Reports

Reports keeps every report generated in this workspace, by you, an agent or an automation, with its key metrics, highlights and data. You can generate a new one, download it or send it after an owner's approval.

## Generate a report

1. Open **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Generate a report**, pick the **Report**, the **Period** and **Who can see it** (**Shared with the workspace** or **Private to me**).
3. Click **Generate now**. The new report opens.
4. To repeat it, choose a **Schedule** and click **Schedule it**. **Manage schedules** opens the automation that runs it.

## Find and download a report

1. Type in **Search** (title or summary), or filter by **Type** and **Created by**, then click **Search**. **Clear** resets the filters.
2. Click a report title to open its metrics, highlights and data.
3. Click **Download CSV**, **Download Markdown** or **Print or save as PDF**. **Copy link** copies a link to the report.
4. If you manage the report, rename it with **Save title**, use **Share with the workspace** or **Make private**, or **Delete** it.

## Send a report by email or WhatsApp

1. Open the report and go to **Share**.
2. Choose **Email** (pick the **From mailbox** and the recipients) or **WhatsApp** (pick a conversation open for replies).
3. Add a **Message** if you like and click **Ask to send**.
4. An owner approves it in Approvals, and only then is it sent. **Open Approvals** takes you there.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members: generate and schedule reports, and ask to send them (with email or WhatsApp access).
- Viewers: read, search and download reports.
- Renaming, sharing and deleting are for the report's creator, or an owner for a shared report. Only the creator can make a report private.
- Sending always waits for an owner's approval.
