# Demo workspace

A workspace called Demo, in your own organization, filled with clearly labelled sample data: tasks, approvals, email, calendar, business records, marketing drafts, files and knowledge. Every sample record starts with "Demo:". Nothing is ever sent from it.

## Create the demo workspace

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar under **More**.
2. Stay on the **Workspace** tab and find **Sales demo workspace**.
3. Select **Create demo workspace**.
4. Wait for the card to show how many sample records it holds.

## Open it for a demo

1. On the **Sales demo workspace** card, select **Open demo workspace**.
2. Every page now shows the banner that this is sample data only.
3. Walk through Tasks, Approvals, Email, Calendar, Business, Marketing, Files and Knowledge Base.
4. Use the workspace switcher in the top bar to go back to your real workspace.

## Reset the sample data

1. Open **Settings**, **Workspace** tab, **Sales demo workspace**.
2. Select **Reset demo data**, then confirm with **Reset demo data**.
3. The sample records are deleted and created again. Records people added to the Demo workspace themselves stay.

## Who can do this

- Only a workspace owner can create, reset or open the demo workspace from this card. Members and viewers see a note that an owner must do it.
- Sending is off for everyone in the demo workspace: email, campaigns, WhatsApp messages, calls and social posts never leave ANUM.
