# Email

The Email Center is your own mailbox on the workspace mail server: folders, search, reading, replies and new messages. Nothing is sent until an owner approves the exact message. The server operator must turn it on first.

## Connect your mailbox

1. Open **Email** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Connect your mailbox**, type your **Email address** and the **App password** from the mail server.
3. Optionally fill **Your name for outgoing mail (optional)**.
4. Click **Connect mailbox**. To connect another one later, use **Add a mailbox** in **Mailbox settings**.

## Find and organize mail

1. Pick a folder under **Folders**.
2. Type in **Search sender, subject or text** and click **Search**. Use **Show only** with **Unread** or **Starred** to narrow the list.
3. Click a message to read it. Images from the sender are never loaded.
4. Use **Mark read**, **Star**, **Archive** or **Trash** on one message, or tick several and act on them together. **Delete for good** in Trash or Junk waits for approval.

## Write and send a message

1. Click **Write**, or open a message and click **Reply**, **Reply all** or **Forward**.
2. Fill **To**, **Subject** and **Message**. Use **Attach files** or **Schedule** if you need them.
3. Click **Send**. The approval panel shows exactly what goes out.
4. An owner clicks **Approve and send** (or **Approve schedule**), here or in Approvals. Use **Save draft** to finish later.

## Who can do this

- Owners: everything, including approving sending and deleting for good.
- Members: connect their own mailbox, read, organize, write and ask to send, and share their mailbox. They cannot approve sending.
- Viewers: cannot use Email.
- A mailbox is private to the person who connected it. Its owner can share it under **Sharing** in **Mailbox settings**, as **Read only** or **Read and send**.
