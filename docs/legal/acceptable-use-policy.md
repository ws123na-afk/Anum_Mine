## You must not use ANUM to

- break any law that applies to you or to the people you contact, including data protection, telecommunications, anti-spam, consumer protection, export control and sanctions laws;
- send messages, emails, calls or campaigns to people who have not agreed to receive them where consent is required, or to people who have opted out. ANUM keeps a contact opt-out list and refuses sends to anyone on it; do not work around it;
- collect or process personal data you have no right to process, or special categories of data (such as health, biometric, religious or criminal records data) without the basis and safeguards the law requires;
- harass, threaten, defame, impersonate or deceive anyone, including making AI calls or messages that hide that they come from an automated system where the law or the channel's rules require disclosure;
- upload or send malware, or content that infringes someone else's rights;
- probe, scan or test the Service's security without our written permission, except as our vulnerability disclosure policy allows (see the [Trust page](https://{{WEB_HOST}}/trust));
- try to reach another organization's data, get around permissions, approvals, rate limits, model budgets or the runtime's tool controls, or share accounts;
- overload the Service, mine cryptocurrency, or resell it without an agreement with us;
- use AI features to produce content that is illegal, or to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people without human review;
- use the finance features to give regulated investment advice to others, or attempt live trading through the Service.
