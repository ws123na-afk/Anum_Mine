# Favorites

Favorites lists everything you starred. Your favorites are private to you: nobody else in the workspace sees them.

## Star an item

1. Open the item where it lives: a conversation in **AI Chat**, a report in **Reports**, or a collection on the **Collections** tab of **Knowledge Base**.
2. Click the star button next to its title. Its label reads **Add** followed by the item name **to favorites**.
3. Click the star again to remove it from your favorites.

## Find and open a favorite

1. Open **Favorites** in the sidebar, under **More**.
2. Click **All**, or a type such as **Conversation**, **Task**, **Report**, **Collection**, **File**, **Memory**, **Automation** or **Agent**, to filter the list.
3. Click an item's name to open it. Reports and collections open at that item; other types open their page.
4. The list shows 30 items at a time. Click **Show more** to load the next ones.

Your first starred items also appear under **Favorites** on the empty AI Chat home, with **See all** to come back here.

## Remove a favorite

1. Open **Favorites** in the sidebar.
2. Click the remove button (the crossed star, **Remove**) on the item's row.
3. A notice confirms it was removed from favorites. The item itself is not deleted.

## Who can do this

- Every role, viewers included, can star and remove favorites.
- You can only star items you are allowed to read. Someone else's conversation or private report cannot be starred.
- Each person can keep up to 500 favorites per workspace.
