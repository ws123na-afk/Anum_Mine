# ANUM Command

ANUM Command is where you give ANUM orders. You always see the plan first, and nothing runs until you press Start.

## Give an order and start it

1. Open **ANUM Command** in the sidebar.
2. Type in the box (Tell ANUM what you need...), or tap the orb and speak, then check the text.
3. To use documents, press **Files**, then **Choose from Documents** or **Attach a document**.
4. Press **Show the plan** (or Enter).
5. Under **Here is what ANUM will do**, check each step. A step marked **Blocked by policy** must be removed before you can start.
6. Use **Move up**, **Move down**, **Remove** or **Edit the request** if needed, then press **Start**.

## Follow a command and approve its steps

1. The tracker shows the newest command with a timeline of its steps. Pick another one under **Recent commands**.
2. When a step waits, press **Review the approval**, or **Approve all** to approve every waiting step with the payload hash shown.
3. Press **Stop** to stop a running command.
4. When it is done, read the results and next steps, press **Download report**, or **Remove from history**. The tasks it created stay in Tasks.

## Save a command you give often

1. Under **Saved commands**, press **New saved command**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Group** and **What ANUM should do**, then press **Save**.
3. Later, press **Plan it** on the saved command. ANUM shows its plan and waits for **Start**.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members can give orders, start and stop commands and edit their saved commands.
- Viewers can follow commands but cannot create them; the page says so.
- Approving steps needs a workspace owner, or a person an approval route names.
