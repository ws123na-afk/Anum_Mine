# Voice

Voice is a spoken conversation with your assistant, named Anum by default. Ask about your workspace or ask for work to be done; anything risky still waits for your approval on screen.

## Talk to the assistant

1. Open **Voice** in the sidebar.
2. Tap the orb (or hold Space) and speak. When you pause, your message is sent. If the browser blocks the microphone, press **Allow microphone**.
3. Or type in the box at the bottom and press **Send**.
4. Use the **EN** or **عربي** switch to choose the speech language.
5. Turn on the wake pill (Wake on "Anum") to wake the assistant by saying its name while the page is open.

## Hand over work

1. Say what you want done, for example "Create a task to draft the weekly report" or "Ask the Sales agent to follow up with yesterday's leads".
2. The reply shows what was done. Press **Open task** to follow it.
3. If a step needs an approval, press **Open Approvals** and decide there. The assistant never approves, deletes or pays by voice.

## Adjust the page and settings

1. Press **Conversation** to show or hide the written conversation.
2. Press **Mute spoken replies** to read replies without hearing them, or **Full screen** for focus mode.
3. Open **Voice settings** to change **Assistant name**, **Language**, **Reply voice** and **Transcript retention**.
4. Press **Clear conversation** to end the current session.

## Who can do this

- Owners and members can talk to the assistant and hand over work. Creating a task needs a member or owner role, and cancelling a task by voice needs a workspace owner.
- Viewers can read the workspace through other pages, but cannot start voice sessions.
- Approvals are never decided by voice, whatever your role.
