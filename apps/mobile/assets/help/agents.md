# Agents

Agents are AI helpers you build: who they are, how they work and what they may use. Every action an agent takes still goes through this workspace's approvals.

## Create an agent

1. Open **Agents** in the sidebar and stay on the **Agents** tab.
2. Press **Create agent**.
3. Pick a template or **Start from scratch**.
4. Go through **Identity**, **Instructions**, **Skills and tools** and **Autonomy and knowledge**, pressing **Next** after each.
5. On **Review**, check the details and press **Create agent**.

## Give an agent work

1. Open the agent from the list. Use **Search agents** to find it.
2. On **Overview**, under **Give it a task**, describe the work and press **Run task**.
3. Follow it with **See it in Tasks**, or **Open approvals** if it waits for approval.
4. Press **Chat** to ask the agent questions instead.
5. Under the **Memory** tab, type a fact under **Add a memory** and press **Save memory** so the agent always keeps it in mind.

## Change, pause or delete an agent

1. Open the agent and press **Edit**, change the steps you need, then press **Save changes**.
2. Under **Who can use it**, choose **Private** or **Shared with the workspace**.
3. Open **More** and press **Pause** to stop it chatting and running tasks, or **Resume** to turn it back on.
4. To remove it, open **More**, press **Delete agent** and confirm. Its tasks and chats stay in the history.
5. The **Org chart** tab shows your agents and team together.

## Who can do this

- Everyone can see their own agents and the workspace's shared ones, and use them.
- Owners and members can create agents, give them tasks and chat with them. Viewers cannot run tasks.
- Only the agent's creator, or a workspace owner for a shared agent, can edit, pause, delete it or change its memories. Only the creator changes who it is shared with.
