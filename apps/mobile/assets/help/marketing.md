# Marketing

Marketing plans, approves and publishes social media posts in English and Arabic to the accounts connected to this workspace, and runs email campaigns through Brevo. Nothing is published or sent until an owner approves it.

## Connect a social account

1. Open **Marketing** in the sidebar and click the **Accounts** tab.
2. Under **Platforms and accounts**, find a provider marked **ready**, click its **Connect** button and sign in at the platform.
3. Use **Check connection** on a connected account if posting stops working.

## Write and publish a post

1. Click **New post** in the page header.
2. Choose the accounts under **Post to**, pick the **Languages** and write the text for each. **Suggest ideas** drafts ideas with AI.
3. Click the save button, for example **Save 2 drafts** (one draft per account and language).
4. On the **Posts** tab, open the post, click **Schedule**, set **When** (or leave it empty) and click **Ask to publish**.
5. An owner checks the **Payload hash** and clicks **Approve**, on the post or in Approvals. Use the **Calendar** tab to drag posts between days.

## Send an email campaign

1. Click the **Email campaigns** tab. An owner first pastes the Brevo API key under **Connection** and clicks **Check and save**.
2. Under **Drafts**, click **New campaign**, fill in the form and click **Create draft**.
3. Open the draft and click **Ask to send**, then **Ask an owner to send it** or **Ask an owner to schedule it**. Owners can click **Send me a test** first.
4. An owner clicks **Approve and send**. Reports appear under **Campaigns**.

## Who can do this

- Owners: everything, including connecting accounts and Brevo, auto-post, test sends and approving publishing and sending.
- Members: write and edit posts and email drafts, use AI ideas, schedule, and ask to publish or send.
- Viewers: see accounts, posts, campaigns, analytics and reports only.
