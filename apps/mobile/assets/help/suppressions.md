# Contact opt-outs

ANUM never messages the people on this list on the channel they chose. A WhatsApp STOP, or an email with the subject "unsubscribe", adds them automatically, and every send, approval and automation checks the list. Numbers and addresses are stored only as a fingerprint and a masked form.

## Add an opt-out

1. Open **Contact opt-outs** in the sidebar under **Admin**.
2. Under **Add an opt-out**, type the **Phone number or e-mail**. Use the country code for phone numbers.
3. Pick the **Channel** (**Every channel**, **WhatsApp**, **E-mail** or **SMS**) and under **Stop** pick **Every message** or **Marketing only**.
4. Add a **Reason**, for example how and when they asked. It is kept in the audit log.
5. Click **Add to the list**.

## Find or remove an entry

1. Type a full number, email or text into **Search**, pick a **Channel** if you like, and click **Find**. **Clear** shows everything again.
2. Each entry shows the masked contact, channel, what it stops, where it came from and who added it. Click **Show more** for older entries.
3. To let ANUM message a contact again, click **Remove**, fill **Reason for removing** and confirm with **Remove**.

## Export the list

1. Click **Export CSV** at the top.
2. ANUM downloads the list and says which file it saved.

## Who can do this

- Workspace owners and organization administrators: view, search, add and remove opt-outs. A removal always needs a reason.
- Workspace owners only: **Export CSV**. Others see that only owners can export.
- Members and viewers: see a notice explaining who can see the list. Every send still checks it for them.
