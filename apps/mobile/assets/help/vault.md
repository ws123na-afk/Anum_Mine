# Document vault

The Document vault keeps important papers in one place. A new document is **Private**: only you, and the people you share it with, can see it. Workspace owners do not see your private documents.

## Add a document

1. Open **Document vault** in the sidebar and click **Add document**.
2. Pick the file, give it a **Title** and a **Category**, and choose **Who can see it**.
3. Click **Add**. Every file is checked for type, size and malware. If the same file is already in the vault, you can **Open the existing one** or **Add it anyway**.

## Keep dates and get reminders

1. Open the document and click **Edit details**.
2. Fill in the **Issue date** and **Expiry date**. ANUM reminds you before the expiry date (30 days by default), 7 days and 1 day before, and on the day.
3. Type the **Document or account number** if you want it kept. It is stored encrypted and lists show only the last 4 characters; click **Show number** to see it.
4. The **Expiring** tab lists documents that expire soon and those already expired. When you renew one, click **Add a new version** on the document.

## Scan and confirm

1. A workspace owner turns scanning on in **Settings**: receipts and invoices, and separately the dates of IDs, passports and insurance.
2. Add a receipt, or open one and click **Read with scanning**.
3. Open **Review queue**. Check each proposed value, correct it if needed, then click **Confirm values** or **Reject**. Nothing is saved until you confirm, and a receipt never adds an expense on its own.

## Share, link and delete

1. On a private document you added, choose a person under **Shared with** and click **Share**. They can open and download it, not change it.
2. Under **Belongs to**, link the document to a vehicle, person, business or purchase.
3. Click **Move to trash** to delete it. Restore it from **Trash** within the days your owner set, or click **Delete for good**.

## Scan from your iPhone

1. Open **Settings** in the vault and click **Make a token** under **Scan from your iPhone**. Copy the token: it is shown once.
2. In the Shortcuts app, follow the steps shown to send a photo, a scan or a shared file to ANUM.
3. Click **Disconnect** if the phone is lost.
