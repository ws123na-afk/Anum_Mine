# Calendar

The Calendar is the workspace calendar: meetings, calls, reviews, reminders and focus time, with the next steps ANUM suggests from your calendar, email and approvals. Events live in ANUM, and an owner can connect a Google or Microsoft 365 calendar under **Sync** to keep both in step.

## Add an event

1. Open **Calendar** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New event**.
3. Fill **Title**, **Type**, **Date**, **Starts** and **Ends**, and optionally **Place**, **Meeting link**, **Phone number**, **People** (one per line) and **Notes**.
4. Click **Add event**. ANUM does not invite people on its own: use **Ask to send invitations** on the event and an owner approves it first.

## Find and manage events

1. On the **Agenda** tab, choose **Week** or **Day** and move with **Previous week**, **Today** and **Next week**.
2. Type in **Search title, place or people**, or tick **Show cancelled**.
3. Click an event to see its details, then **Join** a meeting link.
4. Use **Edit**, **Cancel event**, **Restore** or **Delete**. Set each person's reply under **People**.

## Sync with Google or Microsoft 365

1. A workspace owner opens the **Sync** tab and clicks **Connect Google Calendar** or **Connect Microsoft 365 Calendar**, then signs in with the provider.
2. Events from that calendar appear in ANUM, and events you add or change in ANUM are sent there. If both sides changed an event, the later change wins.
3. If the tab says **Reconnect needed** or **Sync error**, follow the line under it, then click **Sync now**.
4. To invite people, open the event and click **Ask to send invitations**. Nothing is sent until an owner approves the exact event and list under **Approvals**.
5. **Disconnect** stops syncing and keeps the events in ANUM.

## Act on next steps

1. Open the **Next steps** tab.
2. Read the suggestions: preparing for a meeting, following up on missing replies, or a summary after a meeting.
3. Click **Start task** to run a task that drafts only, or **Open** to see the related item. Anything sent waits for approval.

## Who can do this

- Every role, viewers included: see the calendar and the next steps.
- Members and owners: add events and start next-step tasks.
- The person who added an event, or a workspace owner: edit, cancel, delete it and record replies.
