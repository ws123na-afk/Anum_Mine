# Team

Team work is where you assign work to people, ANUM and department assistants. Assignees are notified, overdue work gets a reminder, and you can call a teammate from here.

## Assign work

1. Open **Team** in the sidebar.
2. In **Ask ANUM to assign work**, type who should do what and by when, for example "Ask Ahmed to call the supplier by Thursday", and click **Check**.
3. Review **Title**, **Due** and **Assigned to** (pick a person under **Who do you mean?** if asked), then click **Assign and notify**.
4. For more detail, click **New work item**, fill **Title**, **Assigned to**, **Priority**, **Due**, **Details** and **Checklist**, then click **Create and notify**.

## Follow work through

1. Open **My tasks** for work assigned to you, or **Team dashboard** for everyone's work and the **Workload** per person.
2. On **My tasks**, filter with **Status** and **Sort by**, or search. On the dashboard, pick a name under **Workload** to see only their work.
3. Open an item to change its **Status**, tick checklist items, attach files and **Post** a comment.
4. For an item assigned to ANUM or an assistant, click **Start the agent task**. Risky steps wait in Approvals.
5. **Activity** shows assignments, comments, reminders and assistant actions.

## Call a teammate

1. Open the **People** tab.
2. Click **Call** or **Video** next to a person.
3. During the call use **Mute**, the speaker and camera buttons, and **Hang up**. After it ends, **Call again** is offered.
4. The **Team calls** tab lists **All calls** or **Missed** calls, with **Call** and **Video** to call back.

## Who can do this

- Every role, viewers included: read work items, the dashboard, workload, activity and people, and make team calls.
- Members and owners: create and assign work and comment.
- The creator, the person assigned, or an owner: change an item. The creator or an owner: delete it.
- Everyone edits their own profile on **People**; owners can edit anyone's.
