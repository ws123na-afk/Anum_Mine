# Business

Business is your internal sales desk: the products you sell, the companies you want to sell to, the deals in progress, proposals and the projects that follow. Nothing is sent from here.

## Add a lead

1. Open **Business** in the sidebar and click the **Leads** tab.
2. Click **New lead**, or **Import CSV** to bring in a prepared file (click **Check the file**, then **Import**).
3. Enter the **Company name** and, if you have one, a contact with a **Consent basis**. Only add details you obtained lawfully.
4. Pick an **Offer** from your catalog and click **Add lead**. Check any **Possible duplicates** shown first.
5. Find leads later with the search box and **Find**, or filter by **Status** and **Sort**.

## Turn a lead into a proposal

1. Open the lead and use the **Mark** buttons to move it on, for example **Mark Qualified**.
2. Click **Open an opportunity**. On the **Pipeline** tab, enter your value and probability estimates and click **Save**.
3. Click **Create proposal**, give a **Proposal title** and click **Create proposal** again.
4. On the **Proposals** tab, fill in scope, price lines and milestones, then click **Save**.

## Record an accepted proposal

1. Open the proposal and click **Ask for acceptance**. The version is locked and sent to Approvals.
2. An owner checks the **Payload hash** and clicks **Approve acceptance**, here or in Approvals. **Reject** returns it to draft.
3. Once accepted, click **Start the project**, then follow milestones on the **Projects** tab.

## Who can do this

- Owners: everything, including the **Catalog**, the research caps under **Settings**, deletions, clearing **Do not contact**, and approving acceptance.
- Members: add and work leads, opportunities and proposals, and ask for acceptance. They cannot manage the catalog or approve.
- Viewers: cannot open Business, because it holds contacts' personal data.
