# What ANUM learned

ANUM learns from your ratings, the drafts you edit before sending, your approval decisions and your retries. What it learns is for you alone in this workspace, and you can review, edit or delete any of it here.

## Turn learning on or off

1. Open **What ANUM learned** in the sidebar, under **More**.
2. Under **Learning from your activity**, tick or clear **Learn from my activity**. The badge shows the current state.
3. If learning depends on chat memory, click **Open chat memory in Settings**.

## Correct your style and preferences

1. In **Your writing style**, set **Tone**, **Length**, **Greeting**, **Sign-off** and **Language**, or leave **Use what was learned**.
2. Under **Language per contact**, click **Add a contact** to fix the language for one person. Add **Notes for ANUM** if needed, then click **Save my style**.
3. To start over, click **Reset style**, then **Forget my style**.
4. In **Learned preferences**, pick what it **Applies to**, type the **Preference** and click **Add preference**. Use the pencil or bin buttons to edit or delete one.

## Act on routines and weekly suggestions

1. In **Routines**, click **Find routines** to spot work you repeat at about the same time.
2. Click **Create paused automation**, check it with **Open in the Workbench**, then click **Start the schedule**. Click **Not a routine** to dismiss one.
3. In **Weekly improvement notes**, click **Apply** or **Dismiss** on each suggestion. Click **Refresh this week** to look again. Nothing changes until you apply a suggestion, and every change is audited.
4. In **Feedback ANUM learned from**, filter or search the signals, click **Show text** to read one, and delete any you do not want used.

## Who can do this

- Every active member of the workspace, viewers included, has their own learning page. Nobody else, not even an owner, can see it.
- Members and owners only: create automations from routines.
- Owners only: apply suggestions that change the workspace autonomy (approval policy).
