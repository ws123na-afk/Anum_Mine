# Phone calls

Phone calls give the workspace a shared phone book and call log. Calls go through your own phone for free; once an owner connects a voice provider, you can also call through ANUM and ask for AI calls, which an owner approves one by one.

## Add a contact

1. Open **Phone calls** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add contact** in the header.
3. Fill **Name** and **Phone number**, and optionally **Company**, **Email** and **Notes**.
4. Click **Save contact**. Find it later on the **Contacts** tab with **Search contacts** or **Favourites only**.

## Make a call

1. Open the **Dial** tab, or open a contact on the **Contacts** tab.
2. Type the **Number to call** or use the dialpad.
3. Click **Call on your phone**, or **Call through ANUM** when a provider is connected.
4. For a call through ANUM, tick **Record this call** only if recording is allowed, then click **Start call**. Use **Mute** and **Hang up** during the call.

## Log a call and follow up

1. Click **Log a call** in the header, or log the call when ANUM asks after you hang up.
2. Pick the **Contact** or type the **Number**, then set **Direction**, **Outcome** and **Duration (minutes:seconds)**.
3. Write **Notes** and click **Save and summarize** (or **Save to the log**).
4. On the **Recents** tab, open the call to read the **AI summary**, write a **Next step** with a **Due date**, and click **Save follow-up** or **Save as task**.

## Who can do this

- Owners: everything, including connecting the provider on the **Provider** tab, recording AI call consent, approving AI calls and changing anyone's contacts or calls.
- Members: read contacts and the log, add contacts, call, log calls, summarize, save follow-ups and ask for an AI call. They change only what they added.
- Viewers: cannot use Phone calls.
