ANUM MINE headquarters: third party 3D models

These files are loaded lazily by the 3D scene (apps/web/src/lib/hqModelLayer.ts). They were reduced
for the web (meshopt compression, smaller WebP textures, fewer triangles, extra clips and lights
removed); the artwork is otherwise unchanged. If a file is missing or fails to load, the scene keeps
drawing its built in procedural furniture and people.
