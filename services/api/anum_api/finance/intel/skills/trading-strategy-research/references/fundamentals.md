# Fundamental analysis and valuation

ANUM's strategies use prices only. Fundamentals are explained for context and are not computed, because ANUM has no licensed source of company financials yet.

- **Valuation multiples**: price to earnings, price to book, enterprise value to EBITDA. Cheap on a multiple can mean cheap or troubled.
- **Discounted cash flow**: the value of expected future cash flows discounted at a required return; very sensitive to growth and discount-rate assumptions.
- **Factors**: size and value help explain the cross-section of U.S. share returns (Fama and French 1993); factor premia vary over time and markets.
- **Quality of data**: restatements, different accounting standards (IFRS in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, U.S. GAAP), and reporting delays all matter. Look-ahead bias is common when a backtest uses financials before their publication date.

Before any fundamental rule is added, ANUM needs a licensed data source with point-in-time history (the owner's action).
